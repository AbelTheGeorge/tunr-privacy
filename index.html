Privacy Policy for Tunr

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Tunr does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The microphone is used only to hear the note you are playing, on your device, and nothing it hears is ever recorded or sent anywhere.

Information we collect

None. Tunr has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no servers of its own. The app does not have permission to use the internet at all, so it cannot send anything off your device. Every feature works offline, including in aeroplane mode.

The microphone

The tuner asks for permission to use the microphone so it can measure the pitch of the instrument you are tuning. The sound is analysed as it arrives, a fraction of a second at a time, and is then discarded. It is never recorded, saved to a file, or shared with the developer or any third party.

The microphone is only used while the tuner is on screen. Declining the permission is fine: the metronome and the chord library work without it.

Your settings

Your preferences, such as the theme, favourite instruments, custom tunings and metronome tempo, are stored only on your device. If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include these settings in your personal backup to your Google account, so they come back if you reinstall the app. That backup is handled by Android and Google, not by Tunr, and the developer has no access to it.

Permissions we use, and why

  • Microphone — to hear and measure the note you play while tuning. Nothing is recorded.
  • Vibration — for the small haptic ticks when you tap controls, which you can turn off in the app.

Children's privacy

Tunr does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
